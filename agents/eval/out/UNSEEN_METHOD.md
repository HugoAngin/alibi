The unseen evaluation is owned by the Verify unseen data workflow chat. The master coordination chat owns the public service/probe handoff. Exactly one evaluator capture may open the prepared pack. The application worker never receives gold, case-family metadata, lockbox authority or access to another worker's files.

The evaluator harness commit is bound separately from the unchanged application candidate. Never repoint or relabel an earlier probe receipt.

Use four workers on this machine, each with a disposable candidate archive and fresh scratch state. Per-case limits are 180 seconds for agents, 90 for one-pass, and 30 for rules. Retry evidence reduces concurrency to two; successful retry logs cannot distinguish 429 from 503, so this throttle is conservative. Lead extended the hard stop to 12:50 UTC. If the capture is still running at 12:05, the coordinator writes a scored INCOMPLETE snapshot from completed predictions, fills pending RFCs as unavailable to retain full denominators, and binds all snapshot evidence by hash. This is part of the same authorized capture; replaying that snapshot never launches a model. Stop new launches and bound running processes to the remaining time. Preserve missing, timed-out, invalid and refused outcomes; do not retry hidden cases. Infrastructure exceptions stop queued work and retain evidence for investigation.

The headline scores investigation/draft-evidence correctness from top-level claims. Report differences in `package_claims` separately by RFC and observed reason. Field restrictions and leak checks concern the final package and do not make the headline a proof of recipient correctness after policy filtering. Keep the official and second-scorer results separate, including disagreements.
