## Decisions and deviations

- **Bare request ids (S1 commits 8804ec3 and f6fba51 cherry-picked).** The S1 branch's second
  commit made every holder call without `audience=` raise `TypeError`. The app, the demo cases and
  teammates' acceptance tests (`agents/eval/test_redteam_core.py`,
  `agents/execution/test_dispatch_conformance.py`) call `run_investigation` and `build_review`
  with bare ids, so the merged rule keeps both behaviours: a bare id resolves while exactly one bank
  has used it; once several banks have, the public API raises `AmbiguousRequest` and `audience=`
  names the bank; the store resolves a bare id to the request received first, so a later bank never
  captures an earlier one's drafts (eval's `test_independent_banks_can_reuse_their_own_external_request_id`).
  Store key is `(issuer, request_id)` with `audience=` accepted as an alias; a schema-2 database is
  migrated on open rather than refused (restarts must keep history). `alibi/CONTRACTS.md` and
  `alibi/REPORTS/S1.md` say so.
- Subject binding lives in the signed `case_ref` (now a digest over audience, root request and
  subject) rather than a new `PackageScope` field, because eval's native verifier
  (`agents/eval/native_verify.py`) rejects any extra key in body or scope. Packages signed before
  this change carry the old `case_ref` and fail a subject-bound verification.
- `Claim.gap` is now a code; the app shows `gap_detail` (the sentence) and `gap_code` passes pinned
  codes through. The analyst package template still prints `PackageClaim.gap` (code) in one place
  (research-owned template; the sentence is appended to `rationale`).

## Item 2: adapter

`alibi/adapter.py`: `run_case(case_without_gold, mode, as_of=None) -> [prediction]` per
`alibi/eval/predictions.md`, modes `rules | one_pass | agents`. Each case gets a fresh encrypted
passport seeded through the real connector paths in process: the mock merchant pushes signed
webhooks into `webhook_sink`, the mock bank issues the RFCs into `rfc_sink`, the feed client syncs
the bank rows with its cursor, imports go through `import_self_supplied` (all via
`alibi.connectors.clients.bootstrap`). Each RFC is answered at its own `issued_at`; when a case's
RFCs are issued at different times the services' clocks advance between them and the newly visible
pushes, rows and imports land first, never later data (`test_adapter.py::test_no_lookahead...`).
`alibi.api.run_investigation` now dispatches on mode (`agents` -> `alibi.agents.runner`,
`one_pass`/`single_call` -> `alibi.baselines.one_pass`, NEEDS 1); the Gemini client is
`GeminiClient.for_run(system, run_id)` scoped per case and wrapped by `alibi.agents.replay`
(`ALIBI_REPLAY=1` serves `alibi/fixtures/replays/case<n>_<mode>.json`). Emitted per request:
links, claims (pinned gap code, typed facts, gold-syntax refs), the policy-filtered package body,
the private exclusions, the pre-policy `proposed_package`, cost and retrieval counts, a typed
failure. CLI:


## Item 3: demo runner and the integrated app

`uv run --frozen python -m alibi.demo --timeout 600 [--case 1] [--reset] [--as-of <utc>] [--replay]`
runs the mock merchant (8101), the mock bank (8102) and the holder app (8100) in one process. The
merchant pushes to `POST /connect/merchant/webhook` (loopback only; the issuer signature is the
authentication), the bank forwards every issued RFC to `POST /connect/bank/rfc` with the recipient's
bearer, the app syncs the feed through `POST /connect/bank/sync`, and the runtime reports
`seed: "connectors"` (nothing is preloaded from the case file). Dispatch posts the signed package
to the bank service's `POST /deliver` with the recipient's bearer and stores the nested receipt; the
analyst console reads the inbox, package, dispositions and audit from the bank service and records
dispositions there (a follow-up is a `request_more` disposition; there is no separate follow-up
route). One tokens file (`data/alibi/bank_tokens.json`: `bank_a`, `bank_b`, `feed`, `admin`) serves
the app and both services. One key spec (`CONNECTOR_KEYS == APP_KEYS`) means a record signed under
`mock_marketplace` or `merchant_mock` verifies on the bank service. A bank bearer on a holder route
is refused `403 recipient_credential` (eval's SP-21). Driver subcommands: `python -m alibi.demo status`,
`advance --to <utc>` (both services then the feed sync), `rfc bank_a|bank_b`. Without `--reset` a
restart keeps the passport and the bank state (all duplicates); `--reset` deletes
`data/alibi/demo/case_<n>/` and the bank state and says so. The exact demo sequence with the five
[verify] answers is in `alibi/REPORTS/S5_demo_notes.md`; the live 8100 run is in "Item 4".

Data's QA P0s: (1) and (2) are the template patch (above); (3) a Send button appears in the
approval card after approval and posts to `/requests/{id}/dispatch`; (4) the dispatch page before
any attempt is "Not yet sent" (`outcome: not_attempted`), never "refused".

Known limits: the unexplained-credit alert is due 24 h of app clock after the feed row's
`retrieved_at`, so under the connectors seed it appears after an `advance` of a day, not at the
booking; the bank service's clock must be at or past the app's clock before the first dispatch or
`/deliver` refuses `issued in the future` (the runner advances it); one browser profile per
recipient, because the analyst cookie trips the holder gate.
